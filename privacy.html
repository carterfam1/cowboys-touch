Privacy Policy — Cowboy's Touch

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Cowboy's Touch is a personal helper app by Carter Developments, used only by its owner on one Windows laptop. This policy explains what it accesses.

What the app accesses

The app connects to the owner's Google Drive account to read and write files in one folder only: the "Jewel-Eyes-Hands Relay" folder used to send task files and screenshots between the owner's phone and laptop. It does not read any other Drive files.

What is stored

A Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's laptop so the app can reach Drive without signing in each time. The token is never sent anywhere except to Google, never shared, and never uploaded.

What is not collected

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no other users.
