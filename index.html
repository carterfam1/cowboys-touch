Cowboy's Touch

Cowboy's Touch is a personal helper app by Carter Developments. It runs on one Windows laptop and automates routine steps while building Unity games (Dodgeball By Dogeball and Dogeball's Crypto Wallet): taking screenshots, clicking and typing on screen, and checking build results.

It is controlled by its owner through a Google Drive folder relay. The app is for the owner's personal use only. It is not sold, shared, or offered to the public.
